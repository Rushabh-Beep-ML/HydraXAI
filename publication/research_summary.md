# HydraXAI: Research Summary

## 1. Project Vision
A physically-grounded, interpretable AI framework designed to predict flood risk in the Western Ghats by fusing atmospheric and cryospheric data.

## 2. Methodology
* **Data Fusion:** Integrated satellite-derived elevation (SRTM) with real-time weather observations.
* **Model Pipeline:** LightGBM optimized with Spatial-Block Cross-Validation to prevent spatial bias.
