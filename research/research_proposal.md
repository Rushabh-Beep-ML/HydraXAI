### 1. Abstract
This proposal outlines the development of HydraXAI, an explainable machine learning framework designed to mitigate flood risk in the Indian Himalayan Region. By integrating multi-source remote sensing data with spatial-block validation and TreeSHAP-based interpretability, this project addresses the critical need for transparent, physically grounded disaster decision-support tools.

### 2. Introduction & Problem Statement
Current flood forecasting systems often treat regional climate modeling as a "black box," focusing exclusively on predictive accuracy. This opacity limits the ability of disaster management stakeholders to intervene effectively. There is an urgent need to transition toward "Explainable AI" (XAI) that provides not just a probability score, but the physical drivers (e.g., topography, soil moisture) behind a risk assessment.

### 3. Research Questions
* **RQ1:** To what extent does spatial-block cross-validation mitigate predictive bias caused by geographic autocorrelation in climate data?
* **RQ2:** How can TreeSHAP-based interpretability quantify the physical drivers of flood risk in high-altitude Himalayan basins?
* **RQ3:** Can an explainable intelligence framework provide actionable decision support that is superior to standard binary risk classification?

### 4. Methodology
* **Data Fusion:** Ingestion and normalization of MODIS (vegetation), SRTM (topography), and local precipitation datasets.
* **Spatial Rigor:** Employment of block-k-fold cross-validation to ensure model generalizability across diverse Himalayan terrains.
* **Interpretability Layer:** Utilizing TreeSHAP to decompose complex model outputs into global and local feature importance.
* **Benchmarking:** Comparing HydraXAI's performance against standard, non-interpretable Gradient Boosting models.

### 5. Expected Contributions
* **Academic:** A novel framework for spatial-aware and explainable flood risk intelligence.
* **Practical:** An interpretability audit tool for regional disaster management teams to translate complex AI predictions into actionable evacuation strategies.
